# Haunted Sweden — Product Vision

> **Brand:** The product is always **Haunted Sweden** — see [BRAND.md](./BRAND.md). Never use “Swedish Hauntings” or similar variants in copy or data labels.

## North Star

Haunted Sweden is **not** trying to become the largest haunted-location database immediately.

The goal is to become the **most trusted and user-focused paranormal exploration platform in Sweden** — like *Google Maps for haunted locations*, not a sensational blog or prank app.

## Product Principles

### 1. User First

- **The map is the main product.** Discovery starts on the map.
- Users should find haunted locations **immediately**.
- Avoid blog-style navigation; prioritize **exploration**, not articles.
- Mobile-first, dark, modern, trustworthy, explorative.

### 2. Verification System

Three trust tiers (stored in English in data):

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `community-submitted` | Added by a user; not yet independently verified. |
| `community-verified` | Multiple reports, photos or confirmations from different users. |
| `haunted-sweden-verified` | Visited and documented by the Haunted Sweden team. |

Trust beats volume. Labels are always shown honestly.

### 3. Community (Future)

Prepared in the data model, **not built in MVP**:

- User reports, photos, videos  
- User ratings  
- User investigations  

### 4. Discovery (Future Filters)

Prepared for the map:

- Haunted Sweden Verified  
- Nearby Me  
- Family Friendly  
- Public Access  
- Night Access  
- Free Access  
- Overnight Stay Available  

### 5. Local First (Phase 1)

First expansion focus: locations within **~1 hour of Gothenburg**.

Goals per location:

- High quality  
- Real investigations  
- Original photos and videos  
- Authentic documentation  

### 6. Premium (Future — Do Not Build Yet)

Ideas only: private investigation journal, live investigations, events, badges, exclusive reports.

### 7. Verified Location Content (Future)

Every team-verified place should eventually include:

- Investigation timeline  
- Team notes  
- Investigation media  
- Historical sources  
- Community evidence  

## Design Direction

**Should feel:** dark, modern, mobile-first, trustworthy, explorative.

**Should not feel:** cheap ghost-detector toy, prank app, sensationalist tabloid paranormal site.
